What these statistics can and cannot tell us

Shots, first-half results and cards can help describe how teams perform, but are not automatically causal or predictive. Missing values are excluded from each average, not counted as zero. A team must have enough comparable matches to draw meaningful conclusions.

Possession, xG, player injuries and lineups are not provided in these standard CSV fields. Historic odds are present in some source files but are not imported or used by this module.

Fair prices & market disagreement

Reading an odds comparison

Raw probability: 2.00 decimal odds imply 50% before bookmaker margin. These implied probabilities usually total more than 100% because of overround.

No-vig probability: Each raw implied probability divided by their combined total. This removes the simple proportional margin; it does not reveal a true objective probability.

Bookmakers may differ on rules, limits and market definitions. We do not connect users to gambling transactions or rank wagers by expected profit.

Walk-forward backtest

Predictions are calculated chronologically using only matches known before kick-off, so later results cannot leak into earlier forecasts. The baseline is historical home/draw/away frequency.

Transparent model methodology

1. Calculate each team's historical goals scored and conceded, separated into home and away matches. 2. Shrink uncertain rates toward league averages using eight pseudo-matches, reducing small-sample overreaction. 3. Estimate home and away expected goals with team attack/defence multipliers. 4. Use independent Poisson distributions to produce a score probability matrix and sum it into match-result, goals and BTTS markets. 5. Audit earlier forecasts using a time-ordered backtest. Model limitations include correlation between goals, squad news, red cards, fixture congestion and evolving team strength.

This prototype is for independent statistical analysis. No financial transactions, gambling accounts, betting links, real-money staking tools or promotional gambling content are provided. Data providers' terms and applicable UK advertising rules should be reviewed before wider commercial release.
